Three questions for every sample

Good analysis is a sequence: observe the sample, measure its important properties, then interpret the numbers against a reference.

01

What is in it?

Look for colour, odour, turbidity and dissolved substances. These first observations guide the rest of the work.

02

How much is there?

Use a calibrated pH meter, hardness method, conductivity/TDS meter or approved test method for a numeric result.

03

Is it acceptable?

Compare the result with the relevant standard. A reference value is a guide, not a substitute for a certified report.

Experiment menu

Build your water analysis

These activities are arranged from simple observation to quantitative analysis. The laboratory focuses on direct water-quality measurements.

◉Foundation

pH measurement

Calibrate the pH meter, rinse the electrode, measure the sample and classify it as acidic, neutral or alkaline.

◌Complexometric

Total hardness

Study calcium and magnesium ions and report hardness as mg/L of calcium carbonate (CaCO₃).

▦Instrumental

TDS and conductivity

Use conductivity as an indicator of dissolved ionic material and interpret the meter’s estimated total dissolved solids.

▤Physical test

Turbidity

Learn how suspended particles scatter light and why a clear-looking sample still needs an instrument check.

≋Ions

Chloride and sulphate

Compare common dissolved anions using the approved method supplied by your laboratory manual.

⌁Nutrients

Nitrate and fluoride

Discuss health relevance, calibration curves and careful sample handling for low-concentration parameters.

Standard values at a glance

Commonly used drinking-water values based on BIS IS 10500:2012. “Acceptable” is the preferred limit; “permissible” applies when no alternate source is available.

How to use this table

Record the observed value, unit, method and date. Then compare it with the correct column.

  • Use the same unit as the standard.
  • Check the method detection limit.
  • Do not call simulated data “safe” drinking water.
  • Local regulations and updates may differ.
Parameter Unit Acceptable Permissible Why it matters

Total dissolved solids mg/L 500 2,000 Taste and mineral load
